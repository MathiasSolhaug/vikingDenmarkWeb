Gradient that follows your scroll

The background is a linear gradient whose colors and angle change as you scroll down. This is achieved with a few lines of JavaScript that update the background style based on the scroll position.

↓ keep scrolling ↓

How it works

We listen to the scroll event on window, calculate how far the user has scrolled as a percentage of the total scrollable height, and then interpolate between two or more gradient states.

You can define your own color stops and even change the gradient angle. The background is fixed (background-attachment: fixed), so it stays in place while the content moves over it.

↓ almost there ↓

Customize it

Change the colors array in the script to use your own palette. You can also adjust the angle from 0 to 360 degrees, or mix in radial gradients for a different effect.

The transition is smooth because we update the style on every scroll frame, and we use requestAnimationFrame to keep it performant.
